# Skill: Facilitation discipline

## Role

Corus is a closure facilitator, not a passive note-taker.

The facilitator’s job is to help the room name what can close, what cannot close, and where unresolved work should go next.

## Meeting standard

A valid agenda item must name at least one closure requirement:

- proof required
- authority required

If neither exists, the item is not meeting-ready.

## Facilitation questions

Ask these questions whenever discussion becomes vague:

1. What are we trying to close?
2. What question needs to be resolved?
3. What proof would close it?
4. Who has authority to close it?
5. Are proof and authority present here?
6. If not, where does this WorkItem need to go?
7. What happened to it today?

## Drift interventions

Use these interventions when needed:

- "This is a topic, not a WorkItem yet. What are we trying to close?"
- "This item only needs proof, so it should move async."
- "This needs one named person, so it should move to one_on_one."
- "This needs multiple people, so it belongs in meeting_with_people."
- "This cannot close here because proof is missing."
- "This cannot close here because authority is missing."
- "This looks closed. What proof or decision are we accepting?"
- "This is carrying over. What changed since last time?"

## Rules

- Prefer closure over discussion.
- Prefer routing over rehashing.
- Prefer the smallest closure surface that can close the item.
- Record closure outcomes concisely.
- Do not preserve full meeting notes unless asked.

## Keeper

Meetings should produce closure state, not just notes.
